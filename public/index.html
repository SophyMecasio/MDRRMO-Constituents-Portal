Know what to do

Preparedness begins before a disaster.

A few simple steps can help protect you and your family. Follow official instructions and adjust your preparations to the hazards in your area.

Keep important documents protected, know your evacuation route, and make a family communication plan.

01
Prepare an emergency go-bagInclude drinking water, ready-to-eat food, a flashlight, batteries, first-aid supplies, medicines, and essential documents.
02
Know your evacuation planIdentify safe routes and ask your barangay about the nearest officially designated evacuation area.
03
Monitor official announcementsPay attention to updates from the MDRRMO, your LGU, PAGASA, and other authorized agencies.
04
Check on family and neighborsMake a communication plan, especially for children, older adults, and people who may need assistance.

Community reporting

Submit a non-emergency incident report

Use this form to send a community concern or incident report to the MDRRMO office for review. Submission does not mean that responders have been dispatched or that assistance is guaranteed.

Immediate danger? Do not wait for this form. Call your verified local emergency hotline or contact your barangay / emergency responders directly.

Please do not submit passwords, financial information, medical records, or other unnecessary sensitive details. Review the office privacy notice before public launch.
